# WhatsApp Automation

## Overview

WhatsApp Automation helps businesses automate customer communication throughout the buying journey. Instead of manually sending every message, businesses can create automated workflows that send notifications, reminders, confirmations, and support messages based on customer actions.

Sellers Login provides WhatsApp Automation to help ecommerce businesses improve customer engagement, reduce manual work, and deliver faster communication.

---

# Why WhatsApp Automation Matters

Customers expect instant responses and regular updates after placing an order. Manual messaging becomes difficult as business grows.

WhatsApp Automation helps businesses:

- Respond instantly
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Reduce manual communication
- Save time
- Increase operational efficiency
- Deliver a better shopping experience

# Key Features

## Automated Order Notifications

Automatically send WhatsApp messages whenever the order status changes.

Examples include:

- Order Confirmation
- Payment Confirmation
- Order Packed
- Order Shipped
- Out for Delivery
- Order Delivered

---

## Abandoned Cart Recovery

Automatically remind customers about products left in their shopping cart.

Businesses can encourage customers to complete their purchases through personalized reminders.
