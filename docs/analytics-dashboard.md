# Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

Business decisions become more effective when they are backed by accurate data. An Analytics Dashboard provides valuable insights into sales, customer behavior, inventory performance, and business growth.

Sellers Login offers a comprehensive Analytics Dashboard that enables businesses to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), track revenue, analyze customer trends, and make data-driven decisions from a centralized platform.

---

# Why Analytics Matter

Running an ecommerce business without analytics is like driving without a dashboard. Businesses need accurate reports to understand what is working, identify opportunities, and improve overall performance.

Analytics help businesses:

- Monitor business growth
- Track sales performance
- Understand customer behavior
- Measure marketing effectiveness
- Improve operational efficiency
- Make informed business decisions
