# Why WhatsApp Marketing?

Customers expect instant communication.

Instead of waiting for emails or phone calls, businesses can connect directly with customers through WhatsApp for order updates, promotions, reminders, and customer support.

Some common use cases include:

- Order Confirmation
- Payment Confirmation
- Shipping Updates
- Delivery Notifications
- Promotional Campaigns
- Customer Support
- Lead Nurturing
- Abandoned Cart Recovery

# WhatsApp Marketing Features

## Automated Customer Messaging

Automatically send messages when customers perform specific actions such as placing an order or completing a payment.

## Order Updates

Keep customers informed throughout the order lifecycle.

Examples include:

- Order Received
- Order Confirmed
- Order Shipped
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered Successfully

## Promotional Campaigns

Send targeted promotional campaigns to customers based on business requirements.

Examples:

- New Product Launch
- Seasonal Offers
- Festival Discounts
- Coupon Campaigns
- Flash Sales

## Customer Support

Respond quickly to customer inquiries and provide assistance throughout the shopping journey.

Businesses can use WhatsApp for:

- Product Questions
- Pricing
- Delivery Status
- Return Requests
- Payment Queries
